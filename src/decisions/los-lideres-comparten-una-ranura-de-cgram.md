# The possible leaders share one CGRAM slot

## What

**The possible leaders share one CGRAM slot.**

## Why

Only one leads at a time, so giving each one its own palette means keeping
it taken by someone who isn't on screen.

The exception is **the leader who is also an NPC on that map** —Kersh on
maps 1 and 6, Talan on map 3—, who gets their own because both rows are in
use at once.
