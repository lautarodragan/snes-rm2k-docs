# A long case at zero proves nothing

## What

**A long case at zero proves nothing on its own.** Before believing a zero
you have to **see the page compile**, and better still, see it go red with
something broken on purpose.

## Why

The `talan` case was once green without exercising anything —the page
didn't compile, so both sides did nothing— and twelve hundred frames of
zero look very convincing. What gave it away was something unrelated: the
cartridge's list of music tracks went from four to three.
